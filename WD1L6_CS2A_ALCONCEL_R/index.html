Philosophy

I design the way an architect drafts: plan first, then build, then remove anything the plan did not need.

My identity as a developer sits as craft. I want an interface to feel calm and obvious, with strong typography, honest colors, and structure that a visitor can read without instructions.

Every project below taught me something new about that balance, from my first midterm layouts to my final systems.
